## Why two licences

Code and prose are reused differently. MIT is the customary choice for a proof
development someone may wish to import, extend, or vendor into a larger
formalisation; CC BY is the customary choice for a paper someone may wish to
quote, translate, or teach from. Using each where it fits avoids the friction of
forcing one to serve both.
