# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple research-community standard.

Participants must engage with evidence, arguments, and artifacts rather than attacking people. Criticism of mathematics, formalization, interpretation, novelty, or attribution is welcome and should be specific enough to test. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misrepresentation, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

When raising a dispute, identify the exact theorem, line, claim, citation, build step, or artifact involved and state what observation would resolve the disagreement.
